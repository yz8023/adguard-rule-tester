Frequently asked questions

How do I check whether my ad blocker works?

Run the test on this page: it tries to load advertising and tracking elements. Blocked ones mean your ad blocker is working.

What does the ad test show?

How many advertising and tracking requests were blocked versus allowed — that is, your ad blocker's real effectiveness.

Why is some advertising not blocked?

Some formats (native ads, anti-adblock workarounds) get through. Up-to-date filter lists and extra rules help.

Why test the ad blocker when using a proxy and anti-detect?

So ads and trackers do not break your environment isolation or expose multi-account work when using a proxy.
